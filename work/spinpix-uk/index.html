SpinPix UK: an interactive website for a photo booth hire business

SpinPix UK is a family-run photo booth, selfie pod, magic mirror and 360 video booth hire company in West Yorkshire, covering weddings, parties, proms and corporate events across the UK. We designed and built the website.

The business

SpinPix UK hires out five kinds of booth: a selfie pod, a letterbox selfie pod, a magic mirror photo booth, an enclosed photo booth and a 360 video booth. Customers are planning weddings, birthdays, proms, leavers events and work parties, anywhere in the UK.

The challenge

Event entertainment is a visual, emotional and date-driven purchase. People compare a lot of similar suppliers, many of them on their phones, and they want to see the fun before they enquire. A plain brochure site would undersell the product.

The website needed to look as lively as the booths, help visitors work out which booth suits their event, and make it quick to check a date and get in touch.

What we built

  • A bold, neon-themed design that matches the SpinPix logo and the energy of an event.
  • A "find your booth" quiz that asks a few quick questions, such as the occasion and the number of guests, and recommends a booth.
  • An "is your date still free?" date picker that carries the chosen date straight into the enquiry form.
  • An interactive Party Tunnel that responds to scrolling and, on phones, to tilting the device.
  • Dedicated pages for each type of event (weddings, parties, proms and corporate), the booths themselves, a gallery and FAQs.
  • An enquiry form that emails every enquiry straight to the business, with a thank-you page, and tap-to-call throughout.
  • An accessibility statement and privacy and terms pages.

Built to be found

  • A separate page for every route, each with its own title, description, canonical address and social-sharing image.
  • A sitemap and robots.txt generated at build time, and LocalBusiness structured data.
  • A fast, mobile-first build on modern tooling and a global hosting network.
