Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

What will the chatbot know about my business?

We train it on your own content: your website, services, FAQs, policies and any documents you share. It answers from that information, not from guesswork.

What happens if it doesn't know the answer?

It says so honestly and offers to take the visitor's details or pass them to a person, rather than making something up.

Where can the chatbot live?

On your website, and on channels like WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger, so customers can reach you wherever they prefer.

Can it capture leads?

Yes. It can ask qualifying questions, collect contact details and send hot leads straight to your inbox, CRM or phone.

Will it slow my website down?

No. The chat widget loads in the background after your page, so your site stays fast.

How do I keep it up to date?

We set it up so new information can be added easily, and we review conversations to keep improving its answers.
