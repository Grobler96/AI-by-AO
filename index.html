The problem

DecoNetwork runs your orders.
But it doesn't run your business.

Every decorator and print shop we've spoken to has the same blind spots.

Stock you can't see

DecoNetwork allocates stock to orders automatically but never tells you what's actually left on the shelf. You find out when it's already a problem.

📅

No week-ahead view

Your team starts Monday not knowing what's coming. Orders get missed, dispatches are rushed, and customers chase you before you chase them.

👻

Customers going quiet

A regular customer stops ordering and nobody notices for weeks. By the time you realise, they're already using someone else.

🔥

Orders at risk

Shipping deadlines approach and nobody has a single place to see which orders are due today, which are late, and what still needs producing.

📊

No production metrics

How much did you produce this week? Who decorated what? Which customers drove the most volume? You're running blind without the numbers.

⏰

Time spent digging

Every answer requires opening DecoNetwork, running a report, cross-referencing something else. You spend more time finding information than acting on it.
