How much does a website cost in the UK?

Typical 2026 prices for a UK small business. Your exact price depends on the points below, and you get a fixed quote before any work starts.

Type of websiteOne-off buildWhat you get
Simple brochure site£1,000 to £2,500One to five pages, custom design, mobile friendly, contact form, SEO foundations.
Business website£2,500 to £6,000Five to fifteen pages, custom design and copy structure, forms and bookings, analytics, SEO foundations.
Animated and connected website£6,000 to £15,000+Scroll animation, 3D or film style effects, and connections to your CRM, chatbot and automations.
Online shop£3,000 to £12,000+Product pages, checkout, order and stock connections, and shop chat or order update automations.

Prices exclude VAT and are typical ranges, not a fixed price list. Hosting and care typically cost £20 to £150 a month, plus your domain name (around £10 to £20 a year).

What changes the price

  • Number of pages: more pages means more design, content and testing.
  • Animation and 3D: scroll animation and film style effects take more design and build time than a clean static layout.
  • Connections: forms that feed your CRM, bookings, chatbots and automations add build and testing work.
  • Content: if your copy, photos and logos are ready, it is faster and cheaper than writing and sourcing them.
  • Online selling: shops need products, payments, shipping and stock set up properly.

Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

How much does a website cost in the UK?

For a UK small business, a simple brochure site is typically £1,000 to £2,500, a full business website £2,500 to £6,000, and an animated, connected site £6,000 to £15,000 or more. An online shop is typically £3,000 to £12,000 or more. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

What are the ongoing costs of a website?

Typically £20 to £150 a month for hosting and care, plus your domain name at around £10 to £20 a year. You own your domain and your code, so you are never locked in.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can redesign and rebuild an existing site, keep the pages that already rank well, and set up redirects so you do not lose your search visibility.

Do you build online shops?

Yes. We build online shops and connect them to order updates, stock alerts and shop chat. See AI for ecommerce.

Do you use templates?

No. Every site is designed from scratch around your brand, your customers and what you want visitors to do.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take two to four weeks from the first call to launch, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content comes together.

Will my website work well on phones?

Yes. Every site is built to look and work perfectly on phones, tablets and desktops, and to load quickly on mobile connections.

Is SEO included?

Every site launches with the essentials in place: page titles and descriptions, a sitemap, fast loading, structured data, social sharing images and clean, accessible code.

Can my website connect to my other systems?

Yes, that's one of our strengths. Contact forms, bookings and chat can feed straight into your CRM, inbox and automations.

Where is the website hosted?

We usually host on Vercel, which is fast, secure and reliable worldwide. You own your domain and your code.
