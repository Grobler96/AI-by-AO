AI marketing without the buzzwords

Enquiries come in from your website, your phone, WhatsApp and social media, and too many of them go cold because nobody replied fast enough or nobody chased the quote. That is lost revenue you have already paid to earn.

Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

What is AI marketing for a small business?

It means using AI and automation for the repetitive parts of marketing: replying to new enquiries, following up quotes, sending nurture emails, asking for reviews and reporting on results. You stay in charge of the message, and the busywork runs in the background.

Will AI publish content without me seeing it?

No. When we use AI to draft posts, newsletters or product copy, it is written in your tone and sent to you for approval before anything goes out.

Do you do SEO and local search?

We build SEO foundations into every website we make, and we can set up review requests and local landing pages that support your Google Business Profile. We never promise rankings, because nobody can, but we make sure the basics are done properly.

Which tools do you connect?

We build with n8n, which connects to your CRM, inbox, calendar, spreadsheets, forms and most email platforms. If you already use a tool, we will usually be able to plug it in.

Do you work with businesses outside Leeds?

Yes. We are based in Leeds and meet clients in person across West Yorkshire and Yorkshire, and we work remotely with businesses across the UK.
