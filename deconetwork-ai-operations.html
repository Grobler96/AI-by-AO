Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Who is the AI Operations Layer for?

It's built specifically for print and decoration businesses that run on DecoNetwork, from small shops to multi store operations.

Does it replace DecoNetwork?

No. It connects to DecoNetwork and sits on top of it, turning your existing order, production and stock data into live dashboards, alerts and answers.

What is DAN?

DAN is the AI assistant inside the Operations Layer. Ask it questions in plain English, like what your week looks like or which customers haven't reordered, and it answers from your live data.

How long does setup take?

Setup includes connecting DecoNetwork, configuring your dashboards and workflows, and a walkthrough with your team. We'll give you a clear timeline on your demo call.

Can I see it working with my own data?

Yes. On a demo we can show you a live version connected to your DecoNetwork data before you commit to anything.
