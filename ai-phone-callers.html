How much does an AI phone caller cost?

A call answering and messages setup is typically £500 to £1,500 to build, one that books appointments and captures leads £1,500 to £4,000, and a multi-line or outbound setup £4,000 to £10,000 or more. Monthly costs are typically £100 to £1,500 depending on call volume. You get a fixed quote before any work starts.

What are the monthly running costs?

Typically £100 to £600 a month for a small business, rising with the number of call minutes. That covers hosting, the phone number, AI and voice usage, and time spent reviewing calls and improving the agent.

Which businesses is it suited to?

Any business that misses calls or loses time to the phone: trades and home services, clinics and wellbeing practices, professional firms, hospitality and small shops. See our guide on AI phone answering for trades.

Is it better than voicemail or a human answering service?

Voicemail loses most callers. A human answering service works but is charged per call or per minute and usually just takes a message. An AI phone caller answers instantly at any hour, can book appointments and answer questions, and hands over to a person when needed.

Does an AI phone caller sound robotic?

Modern voice agents sound natural, with realistic pacing and tone. We choose a voice that suits your brand and tune how it speaks so callers have a smooth, friendly experience.

Can callers still speak to a real person?

Yes. We set clear rules for when the agent hands over, such as an urgent issue, a complaint or a caller simply asking for a person, and it transfers the call or books a callback.

Can it use my existing phone number?

In most cases, yes. Calls can be forwarded from your current number, or we can set up a new UK number for the agent.

What can it actually do on a call?

Answer questions about your services, qualify enquiries, book appointments straight into your calendar, take messages, and send a summary of every call to your inbox or CRM.

Is it GDPR compliant?

We design every agent with UK GDPR in mind, including telling callers they're speaking to an AI, handling recordings and transcripts carefully, and only storing what's needed.

How long does it take to set up?

A focused receptionist agent can usually be live within one to two weeks, including testing it with real call scenarios.
