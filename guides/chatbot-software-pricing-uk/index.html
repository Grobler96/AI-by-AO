Chatbot software pricing in the UK: a 2026 price list

By the AI by AO team · Published 19 November 2026 · Updated 22 November 2026

Chatbot software pricing is confusing because every supplier charges in a different way. Here is a plain price list for UK small businesses, how the pricing works, what the headline price leaves out and how to get a quote you can compare.

The short answer

Chatbot software typically costs £0 to £30 a month for a free widget, £20 to £150 a month for a small business platform, and £50 to £500 or more a month for an AI-powered support platform. A custom-built chatbot is typically £500 to £10,000 or more to build, plus £30 to £600 a month to run. These are typical ranges, not a quote.

How chatbot software is priced

The same chatbot can look cheap or expensive depending on how it is charged.

  • Flat monthly fee: one price for a set feature list. Easy to budget for, but check the limits.
  • Per seat: you pay for each team member who answers handed-over chats.
  • Per conversation: you pay for each chat, so busy months cost more.
  • Per resolved question: you pay when the chatbot answers without a human. It sounds fair, but costs can climb with volume.
  • Usage-based: you pay for the AI used behind the scenes, plus the platform. Common for custom builds.

What the headline price leaves out

  • Set-up and training: someone has to load your content, set the rules and test it.
  • Integrations: connecting your calendar, CRM or shop is often extra.
  • WhatsApp and messaging fees: channel charges can sit on top of the subscription.
  • Extra seats and overage: going over your plan limits can change the bill.
  • Branding: removing the supplier's logo is often a paid upgrade.
  • Keeping it accurate: a chatbot that is never reviewed drifts out of date, and that takes time or a support plan.

Software or a custom build?

Software suits you if you only need basic answers, you are happy to set it up yourself and you do not need it connected to other systems. A custom build is worth it when you want it trained properly on your business, connected to your calendar, CRM or shop, handed over to your team cleanly and improved over time. How to get a chatbot software quote

Whoever you ask, give them the same brief so the quotes line up:

  • What the chatbot should do: answer questions, capture leads, book appointments.
  • Where it will run: your website, WhatsApp or both.
  • How many conversations you expect each month.
  • What it must connect to: calendar, CRM, inbox or shop.

Then ask for the build cost and the monthly cost separately, what happens if you go over your limits, who owns the chatbot and its content, and whether you can take it with you if you leave. We cover this in more detail in our chatbot cost guide.

Questions people ask

How much does chatbot software cost per month?

Free widgets cost nothing, small business platforms are typically £20 to £150 a month, AI-powered support platforms £50 to £500 or more, and enterprise plans from around £500 a month on a custom quote. A custom-built chatbot is typically £500 to £10,000 or more one-off, plus £30 to £600 a month to run.

Is there a free chatbot software option?

Yes. Free plans exist and are fine for basic questions. They usually limit conversations, add the supplier's branding and offer little training on your own content or handover to your team.

Which chatbot software has a free plan?

Several do. As of 22 November 2026, Tidio, Crisp, Botpress and Landbot each list a free plan on their pricing pages, with limits on conversations, seats or AI features. Chatbase and Intercom list free trials instead. A free plan suits basic questions; check the limits and branding before you rely on one.

What is the difference between chatbot software and a custom chatbot?

Chatbot software is a subscription you set up yourself. A custom chatbot is built and trained for your business and connected to your systems, so you pay a one-off build plus running costs. Software is cheaper to start. A custom build usually works better once you need real integrations.

Why do chatbot prices vary so much?

Because suppliers charge in different ways: per seat, per conversation, per resolved question or a flat fee. Add-ons such as WhatsApp, integrations and extra training change the total. Always compare the total monthly cost at your expected volume.

How do I get a chatbot software quote?

Tell the supplier what the chatbot should do, which channels it needs, how many conversations you expect and what it must connect to. Ask for build and monthly costs separately. We give a fixed quote after a free call.
