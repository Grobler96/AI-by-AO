How much does an AI receptionist cost in the UK?

By the AI by AO team · Published 19 November 2026

An AI receptionist answers your calls, takes messages and books appointments, day and night. Prices range from a few pounds a week for a simple app to a few thousand pounds for a system built around your business. Here is what you actually pay and what changes the price.

The short answer

An off-the-shelf AI receptionist app typically costs £20 to £100 a month. A custom AI receptionist built for your business typically costs £500 to £4,000 or more to build, plus £100 to £600 a month to run, rising with call volume. These are typical UK ranges, not a quote.

What drives the price

  • Call volume: most services charge for call minutes, so a busy line costs more than a quiet one.
  • What it needs to do: answering and taking messages is simple. Booking into your calendar and updating your CRM takes more building and testing.
  • Number of lines or locations: each extra number or site adds set-up.
  • Outbound calls: follow-ups and reminders are priced separately from answering.
  • How well it knows your business: a receptionist trained on your services, prices and rules takes longer to set up than a generic one, but handles far more calls properly.

Costs people forget

  • Call minutes beyond your plan: check what is included and what overage costs.
  • Phone number rental: a new UK number can carry a monthly fee.
  • Recording and storage: keeping call recordings and transcripts can cost extra and has UK GDPR implications.
  • Text messages: confirmations and missed-call texts are usually charged per message.
  • Keeping it accurate: your prices and opening hours change, so someone has to keep the receptionist up to date.

Is it worth it?

Work it out for your own business. As an illustration only: if a tradesperson misses ten calls a month, and three of those would have become jobs worth £200 each, that is £600 a month in lost work. Compare that figure with the monthly cost. If the number is small, a simple app may be enough. If it is large, a properly built AI receptionist can pay for itself quickly. How to compare AI receptionist quotes

Ask every supplier the same questions:

  • How are call minutes counted and what happens if I go over?
  • Can it book into my calendar and update my CRM?
  • When and how does it hand a call to a person?
  • Can I keep my existing phone number?
  • How are recordings and transcripts stored under UK GDPR?
  • Can I try it first, and what is the minimum term?

Questions people ask

How much does an AI receptionist cost per month?

Off-the-shelf AI receptionist apps typically cost around £20 to £100 a month. A custom AI receptionist built for your business usually runs £100 to £600 a month, rising with call volume, plus a one-off build of £500 to £4,000 or more depending on bookings and integrations.

Is an AI receptionist cheaper than a human receptionist or answering service?

Usually, yes. A human answering service is often charged per call or per minute, and a part-time receptionist is a salary. An AI receptionist answers every call at any hour for a fixed monthly fee plus usage, though complex calls still need a person.

Are there extra costs beyond the monthly fee?

Possibly. Check call minutes included, a phone number rental, call recording storage, text message charges and any set-up fee. A fair quote lists all of them separately.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

In most cases, yes. Calls can be forwarded from your current number, or you can use a new UK number for the AI receptionist.
