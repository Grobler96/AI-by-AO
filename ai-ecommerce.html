Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

Which ecommerce platforms do you work with?

We connect to platforms such as Shopify and WooCommerce, plus the helpdesk, accounting, courier and spreadsheet tools around them, using n8n and their APIs. If you are on something else, ask and we will check.

Can a chatbot really answer questions about orders?

Yes, when it is connected to your order system. It can look up an order, give the delivery status and answer common questions about returns and delivery. Anything unusual is passed to a person with the full conversation.

Do you only work with Yorkshire stores?

No. We are based in Leeds and meet Yorkshire clients in person, but everything here can be built remotely, so we work with online shops across the UK.

We run a print or workwear shop. Is there anything specific?

Yes. For DecoNetwork businesses we offer a dedicated AI operations layer with live dashboards, stock intelligence and reorder alerts. See the DecoNetwork page.
