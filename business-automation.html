What is business automation?

Every business has jobs that follow the same steps every time: copying details from an email into a spreadsheet, chasing an unpaid invoice, sending the same welcome pack, building the Monday report. They're important, but they eat hours and they're easy to get wrong when you're busy.

Business automation hands those jobs to software. We design workflows in n8n that watch for a trigger, like a new order, a form submission or a date, and then carry out every step for you across all your apps. Your team stops doing copy and paste admin and spends that time on work that actually needs a person.

Questions people ask

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

What is n8n?

n8n is a workflow automation platform. It connects the apps you already use, such as Gmail, Xero, Shopify, HubSpot or Google Sheets, and moves data between them automatically. It can be self hosted, which means you own your workflows and your data rather than renting them.

Do I need to change the tools I already use?

No. We build around your existing tools. If something you use has an API, or can send and receive email or files, it can almost always be connected.

How long does it take to build an automation?

A single focused workflow is often live within days. Larger projects that connect several systems typically take two to three weeks, including testing with your real data.

What happens if something breaks?

Every workflow we build includes error handling and alerts, so if a connected app changes or goes down you hear about it straight away rather than finding out weeks later. We stay on hand to fix and improve things.

How much does business automation cost?

It depends on what you want to automate. Every project is quoted after a free call where we map out your processes, so you know exactly what you're paying for before anything is built.

Is my data safe?

We only connect what the workflow needs, use secure credentials, and can host n8n on infrastructure you control. Your data isn't used to train AI models.
